Q & A

Frequently asked questions.

Is admission free? ×
Yes, every meeting is free to attend. Reservations are not required.
What can I expect? +
You can expect an inspiring evening of worship, prayer, and healing testimonies. The atmosphere is open and welcoming for everyone.
Can I park? +
Yes, free parking is available at the Congress Centrum 1931. Follow the signs to the parking area on Oude Engelenseweg.
Are accessible parking spaces available? +
Yes, accessible parking spaces are available near the entrance of the building.
Are there EV charging stations for my car? +
EV charging facilities are available at the Congress Centrum parking area.
Is accommodation arranged for me? +
Accommodation is not arranged by the organization. However, there are various hotels nearby in Den Bosch.
